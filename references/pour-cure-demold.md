# Pour, cure and demold

## Before pour day
1. Mold sealed (XTC-3D or epoxy), sanded and inspected. Every seam lined with clay or caulk from the outside.
2. Release applied and dried per the maker (Formcoat WB250: two thin coats, dry to touch).
3. Mold assembled in order, all bolts in, clamps on. Core held down with a **positive hold-down** (bridge + threaded rod to the base). Weights slide; friction caps lift.
4. Level the base. Fill the mold with water to the rim once (leak test) if the mold is waterproof enough to dry quickly, or at least check seams with a flashlight from inside.
5. Pre-weigh every batch: dry mix, pigment, fiber, water reducer in labelled bags; water in marked jugs.
6. Temperature: 60–80 °F (15–27 °C) for mixing, curing and the mold. Below 50 °F the cure stalls; above 90 °F the mix stiffens before the mold is full.

## Mixing (premix, per batch)
1. Water + water reducer + pigment + polymer (if separate) in the bucket. Mix 30 s.
2. Add dry mix gradually with the paddle at low speed. Mix 2–3 min until smooth and lump-free. It should flow like thick pancake batter.
3. Add fiber last, sprinkled in. Mix 30–60 s, no longer.
4. Pour within 15–20 minutes of first water. Mix the next batch while the first is going in.

## Pouring a closed vessel mold
- Pour slowly in one place at the funnel; let it climb the wall. Moving the stream around traps air.
- **Two lifts** if the core is light or its hold-down is in doubt: fill to about one-third, vibrate, wait 20–40 minutes until it stiffens, then fill the rest. The first lift locks the core and halves the uplift on the second.
- Vibrate: tap the outside of the shell with a rubber mallet in rings from bottom to top, or run an orbital sander or a vibration table against the base. Continue until bubbles stop rising at the funnel (often 3–5 minutes per lift). Over-vibrating segregates fiber.
- Fill to the top of the riser. Watch for leaks and pack clay on any wet seam immediately.
- Cover the riser with plastic.

## Curing
- Keep it in the mold, covered, **24–48 hours** at 60–80 °F before demold. Cooler shop = longer.
- With 5–7% polymer no wet cure is needed after demold; wrap loosely in plastic for 3–7 days anyway to keep the colour even and avoid efflorescence.
- Full strength at 28 days. No soil, water or sealing before 7 days; seal after 28.

## Demolding
1. Strike the riser while it's green (8–24 h) if it's planned to be cut off: a sharp trowel or wire.
2. Remove clamps and bolts. Remove pieces in the order on the project page:
   - funnel and cap
   - top band of shell pieces
   - working down to the base
   - the core last, key pieces first
3. Pull each piece straight along its pull. Never pry against the green concrete; pry between flange and flange with a plastic wedge.
4. Stuck piece: wait 6 hours, tap it, use compressed air at the seam. Heat-gun on PETG only gently: it softens.
5. Inspect every mold piece for damage before the next cycle; re-seal any scratched surface.

## Finishing
- Grind parting-line fins with a diamond hand pad (200 → 400 → 800). Work wet.
- Bug holes: slurry made from the same mix (with pigment), minus fiber and sand, rubbed in at 2–4 days, then sanded.
- Drain hole: drill or clean out the pinned hole; chamfer the edge.
- Seal at 28 days: penetrating silane/siloxane first (outdoor protection), then an enhancing or matte sealer for colour. Inside of a planter: a cementitious waterproofer or a planter liner.

## Things that go wrong
| Problem | Cause | Fix next time |
|---|---|---|
| Patchy grey/black | Different water per batch; pigment not dispersed; different cure | Pre-weigh water; mix pigment first; cure every unit the same |
| White bloom | Efflorescence | Metakaolin; cover while curing; penetrating sealer |
| Bug holes on the surface | Trapped air, sticky release | Vibrate more; thinner release; brushed face coat |
| Visible fibers | Thin spot, worn surface | Even wall thickness; brushed face coat without fiber |
| Crack near a thick spot | Shrinkage restraint | Even walls; let the mold or core move; demold on time |
| Mold piece cracked on demold | Undercut, low draft, pried on concrete | Fix draft in CAD (`moldcheck`); pry flange-to-flange |
| Core rose | Uplift beat the hold-down | Bolted hold-down; two lifts |
